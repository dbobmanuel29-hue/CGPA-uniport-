# CGPA+ Public-University Rollout Validation

**Research date:** 11 October 2026  
**Product rule:** A university must not become calculation-enabled just because it is public or uses a familiar grading scale. Enable it only after (1) genuine student need is evidenced, (2) official grading rules are verified for the relevant programme/cohort/session, and (3) its course catalogue has been reviewed. Private universities are intentionally outside CGPA+'s target directory.

## What must happen before enabling any candidate

1. **Need validation:** Collect student responses from the target institution. Ask whether their official portal already computes semester GPA and cumulative CGPA, whether they can forecast future grades, and what is missing. Do not infer absence from a web search alone.
2. **Official rules:** Obtain a current university-wide handbook/regulation or written confirmation from the registrar/academic office. Record grading scale, mark boundaries, credit weighting, carry-over/repeat treatment, classification thresholds, and any programme/session/cohort exceptions.
3. **Catalogue:** Build and review faculties, departments, programmes, levels, semesters, course codes, titles, credit units, prerequisites (if used), and academic-session validity from official programme handbooks/catalogues. Never fabricate course rows to make a catalogue look complete.
4. **Testing:** Test GPA, CGPA, repeated/failed courses, rounding, classification boundaries, and historical cohorts against independently checked examples.
5. **Release gate:** Keep the institution marked coming-soon until evidence and tests are complete. Only then propose enabling it in a separate, reviewable change.

## Evidence status and limitations

This is an initial desk-research record, not proof that RSU, UNN, or UNILAG lack official calculators. Third-party calculator pages establish that alternatives exist, not that students lack official options or that the alternatives are accurate. No institution has been enabled by this research. UniPort remains the only calculation-enabled institution.
